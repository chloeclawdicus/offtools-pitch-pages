/ ɡrɑːft /
Graft
noun, verb — British English
1. Hard work done over time. "He put in the graft."
Graft
noun — Horticulture, Surgery
2. To join something onto something else so it grows as one. To make it part of the whole.
Graft
noun — Carpentry & Joinery
3. The shape cut into a rafter or spar. The joint that holds structure together.

A word with roots in the trade. A name that means something — hard work, craft, and the joining of things to make something better.
