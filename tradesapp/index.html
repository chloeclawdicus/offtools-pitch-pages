Blueprint

Product Designer · Knows how you work

One voice conversation. Blueprint learns your preferred materials, quality standards, day rate, and the jobs you love — then builds intelligent Job Type templates that capture exactly how you approach a media unit, a fitted wardrobe, or a garden room. Every quote and every conversation from that point forward is shaped by your expertise.

Sketch

OnSite Designer · Closes jobs in the room

Used with the customer present. Speak the measurements, describe what they want — Sketch generates a visual render of the finished piece in their actual space, on their phone, in real time. Design changes happen by voice. By the time you leave the house, the client has seen their finished job, agreed on the design, and signed off digitally. Sale made before a single piece of wood is cut.

Live renders Voice-driven changes Digital sign-off
💰

Quote

Estimator · Professional quote before you hit the road

The moment design is agreed, Quote calculates materials quantities, checks live prices from your preferred merchants, applies your day rate and margin, and produces a professional branded quote — ready to send before you've reached the end of the customer's street. It also generates a materials shopping list organised by merchant, showing where each item is cheapest. And it learns: after five garden rooms, quotes are calibrated against actuals, not guesses.
